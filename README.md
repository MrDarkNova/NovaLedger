## What you can do

- Add demo funds in one tap (₦1,000, ₦5,000, ₦20,000) or enter your own amount
- Pay airtime, data, cable TV, electricity or send to a name. Each payment is checked, shown for review, then confirmed, and ends with a receipt
- Move money into and out of the **Ajo pot** (savings are kept, not spent)
- See activity with amounts, references and times
- **Reset demo** wipes the wallet from this device

## Honest limits

- No real naira, BVN, or transfers
- Everything is stored in your browser (`localStorage`). Nothing is sent anywhere
- Amounts are stored in kobo (whole numbers), so totals never drift
- Not SwiftPay. Not a bank.
